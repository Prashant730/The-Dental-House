Our services

Complete dental care under one roof

From routine cleanings to advanced cosmetic work, we keep your smile healthy at every stage.

General & Preventive Dentistry

Routine checkups, cleanings, scaling, polishing and preventive care to maintain your oral health.

Restorative Dentistry & Fillings

Tooth-coloured composite fillings and restorations to repair cavities and damaged teeth.

Root Canal Treatment

Painless endodontic treatment using latest rotary technology to save infected teeth.

Cosmetic Dentistry & Smile Makeover

Veneers, teeth whitening, bonding and complete smile design for a stunning transformation.

Orthodontics (Braces & Aligners)

Metal braces, ceramic braces and clear aligners for perfectly aligned teeth at any age.

Dental Implants

Permanent titanium implants to replace missing teeth — look, feel and function like natural teeth.

Crowns, Bridges & Dentures

Custom prosthodontic solutions — ceramic crowns, fixed bridges and comfortable dentures.

Oral Surgery

Wisdom tooth extraction, surgical removal of impacted teeth and minor oral surgical procedures.

Periodontics (Gum Care)

Deep cleaning, gum disease treatment, flap surgery and comprehensive periodontal care.

Dental Diagnostics & Advanced Technology

Digital X-rays, RVG, OPG and intraoral cameras for precise diagnosis and treatment planning.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle, fear-free treatments tailored for children to build healthy dental habits early on.

Emergency Dental Care

Immediate attention and pain relief for severe toothaches, trauma, or broken teeth.
